## Prepared What to Test / reviewer steps

No account or sign-in is required.

1. Add two or more drinks with different prices and volumes; confirm ranking, price per litre, and highlighted savings.
2. Edit and delete a drink, then test delete-all confirmation and cancellation. Check saved data survives relaunch/update.
3. On Duo, test closed outer and open inner displays: two outer columns at standard text size; narrower highlighted card on the inner display. Switch displays during editing and confirm unsaved input remains intact.
4. On a conventional iPhone, check compact widths, large text, VoiceOver, keyboard focus, tabs, and scrolling. Accessibility text sizes should use a single list column.
5. Check widget data updates after successful edits and deletion, App Clip startup, test ads, and StoreKit sandbox donation outcomes. Do not use real purchases for acceptance testing.

These instructions are prepared locally; they have not been saved to TestFlight or App Store Connect.
